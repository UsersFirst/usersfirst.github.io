We've always believed the best software starts with the people who use it.
That's the whole idea behind the name — **Users First** — and it's the lens we
bring to everything we make, whether that's an AI tool or a physics-driven game.

This journal is where we'll share the work in progress: what we're building, why
we're building it, and what we learn along the way.

## What to expect

A few things you'll find here over time:

- **Build notes** — the decisions, trade-offs, and occasional dead ends behind
  our AI and games projects.
- **Field notes** — small ideas about design, craft, and putting users first.
- **Releases** — when something new ships at `games.usersfirst.com` or
  `ai.usersfirst.com`, you'll hear about it here first.

## How this blog works

Every post here is just a Markdown file in our GitHub repository. When we push a
new file, the site rebuilds itself automatically and the post appears. Posts we
aren't ready to share yet stay marked as drafts — they live in the repo but
never show up on the live site until we flip a single line.

> Simple tools, used carefully, tend to outlast clever ones.

Thanks for reading. There's a lot we're excited to share — and this is just the
first entry.
